Data deletion instructions

TikTok Channel / Chy Vy Znaly · Last updated 12 October 2026

To request deletion of comment information stored by this application, email cyfrovahata@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”.

What to include

  1. The platform: Facebook, Instagram or YouTube.
  2. A link to the relevant comment or post, and your visible username or profile link so the operator can identify the records.
  3. Whether you want a particular comment removed or all stored comments associated with that account removed.

Do not send passwords, access tokens or identity documents. If needed, the operator will ask for minimal additional information to verify that the request concerns your information.

How the request is handled

Requests are reviewed manually by the operator. After identifying the records and verifying the request, the operator removes the relevant application-controlled comment memory, reply drafts and pending event records, and handles corresponding copies in the operator's Telegram chat. The operator will confirm the outcome by email. Records held separately by service providers may require their own deletion process.

This request concerns copies held by the application. To delete the original comment, use the Facebook, Instagram or YouTube interface. If the original remains accessible on a connected account, future monitoring may retrieve it again.

Disconnecting an account

The operator can revoke the application's access through the connected platform's account settings and remove its stored credentials. Revoking access stops further authorised retrieval; it does not itself erase existing application copies.
