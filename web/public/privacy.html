Privacy Policy

TikTok Channel / Chy Vy Znaly · Last updated 12 October 2026

This application is operated by the creator of Chy Vy Znaly to manage the creator's own social accounts, publish content and handle comments. Administrative access is restricted to the operator. Public commenters do not need to register with this application.

Information processed

  • Account identifiers and authorisation tokens for connected Google, Meta and TikTok accounts.
  • Videos, scripts, post descriptions and publication identifiers supplied by the operator through Google Drive and Google Sheets.
  • Comments on the connected accounts: text, author names or usernames available from the platform, comment and post identifiers, timestamps, replies and conversation relationships.
  • Reply drafts, published replies, processing status, webhook events and operational logs.

Why we use this information

The application publishes content, detects new comments, keeps conversation context and prepares relevant replies using the post's script and the comment thread. The operator can review reply drafts through Telegram. Configured automatic reactions or replies may also be applied. Comment history is stored so later replies can take earlier messages into account.

Storage and service providers

Railway hosts the application and its configuration. Google Drive stores content, comment memory and processing state; Google Sheets supplies publication information. Telegram delivers comments and reply drafts to the operator. Relevant comment text, conversation context and post information may be sent to OpenAI to generate replies. ElevenLabs may process scripts to generate narration. Connected social platforms receive published content, replies or reactions.

These providers process information under their own terms and policies. OpenAI's retention and data-sharing treatment depend on the API account's settings and applicable terms. We do not sell comment data.

Retention and deletion

Comment history and cached post context remain stored until removed by the operator; there is currently no automatic expiry for conversation memory. Deleting a comment on Facebook or Instagram does not automatically remove the application's stored copy. Webhook deduplication records are retained for up to seven days, subject to a size limit; pending events remain until processed or removed. Logs and provider-held records are subject to the relevant provider's retention settings.

You can request removal of your stored comment information using the data deletion instructions. Removing application copies does not delete the original post or comment on a social platform.

YouTube API Services

This application uses YouTube API Services to publish the operator's videos and manage comments on the connected channel. Use is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. The operator can review or revoke Google access at Google account permissions.

Access and contact

Tokens are stored in the application's hosting configuration and used to access connected accounts. Administrative controls are restricted to the operator. For questions or a deletion request, contact cyfrovahata@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or access tokens.
