Privacy Policy

Chy Vy Znaly publishing automation · Last updated 28 July 2026

This tool is operated by an individual for their own social media accounts. It has one user — the operator — and collects no information about anyone else.

Information the tool stores

  • Authorisation tokens for the operator's own Google, Meta and TikTok accounts, held as environment variables on the hosting provider and used solely to publish the operator's own content.
  • Content the operator created: video topics, titles, descriptions and finished MP4 files, stored in the operator's own Google Sheet and Google Drive.
  • Publication markers — the identifier a platform returns after a successful upload, stored so the same video is not published twice.

Information the tool does not collect

  • No data about YouTube viewers, subscribers or any other users.
  • No data about channels or videos the operator does not own.
  • No analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising identifiers.
  • No personal data of third parties, and therefore none is inferred, sold or shared.

YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the operator agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Data obtained through the API is limited to the operator's own channel identity and is used only to confirm the upload destination. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be reviewed or revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; revoking it immediately and permanently ends this tool's access.

Service providers

Hosting is provided by Railway. Images and narration are produced with generative services (OpenAI, ElevenLabs) from prompts written by the operator. Finished videos are delivered to the platforms the operator publishes on: YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. No data beyond the operator's own content and authorisation is sent to any of them.

Retention and deletion

Content remains in the operator's own Google Drive and Google Sheet until the operator deletes it. Authorisation tokens are kept only while the integration is in use and are removed when the corresponding variable is deleted or access is revoked. Because no third-party personal data is collected, there is no third-party data to request, export or erase.
